# Planned Batches are derived, not stored

Future (Planned) Batches are always computed from Harvest Targets and Skipped Weeks; only a Batch the grower has actually sown is persisted. This keeps the plan consistent when a target changes (no stale future rows to reconcile) at the cost of not being able to hand-edit a single future Batch — growers adjust by changing the target, skipping a week, or entering different real counts when they Sow.
